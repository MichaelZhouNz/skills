# Screen patterns

Whole-screen recipes at 402×874 pt. Numbers are from the top of the screen. Every recipe ends with the same questions: what is the skim line, where is the answer, which one thing is the button, what folds.

## Onboarding step (set-up, "Step n of 9")
- 54 status · progress row 62 to 106 (caret + segments) · title at 132 · helper "Step 3 of 9. One sentence." 6 under · first control 24 under the helper · footer with Continue.
- One question per step. If a step holds two questions, it is two steps (the region left "Who's buying?" and became step 2).
- The optional step (the co-buyer invite) has Share invite as the button and "Skip for now" as a text button under it, with the sign-in caption above the button.
- Choices are option cards 12 apart; lists of many choices are chips 44 tall, 8 apart, grouped under section headers 24 apart with a one-line caption at most.
- A summary step is one list of rows with chevrons (tap to edit), never five Edit links and a repeated title.

## Intro page (first open, shown once)
- 54 · dots + Skip row 62 to 106 · a 320 pt surface2 panel 24 under (so 130 to 450) · tabTitle 28 under · one paragraph 10 under · footer button ("Next", last page "Continue").
- Four pages at most, one idea each, in the order: what it does, how it is different, what the score means, what it costs. Illustrations are the product's own cards and chips. Sample content uses Sample Avenue, Sample Street, Sample Suburb.
- Runs after the splash and before Welcome. Skip and the last Continue both land on Welcome. Never shown again, and not shown when the app opens from an invite link or a share.

## Welcome (dark by design)
- The mark (120) and the app name centred in the upper space; the title in tabTitle, the helper, "Get started" filled, then two text buttons ("I have an invite code", "I already have an account"), 34 above the bottom.

## Tab screen
- 54 · tabTitle at 68 with up to two 44 icon buttons at the right · helper 6 under · content from 24 under · a 96 pt tab bar.
- Home: hero card first (the next thing to do), then "Top fits" header with the Known gloss as its caption, rows, then the ad card.
- Shortlist: helper, segmented List/Map 16 under, the Known gloss once, compact cards (a hero-sized thumb is allowed here), Compare pinned above the tab bar.
- Map: the control, then the map to 16 above the tab bar, pins in 44 targets, one credit pill.
- Plan: day chips, a reminders caption row, stops as time + card with a travel chip between, the one filled button ("Show route on map"), a text button, a disclosure row for settings.
- Priorities: the ranking as rows, then the points list as stepper rows, the tier chip and the total on one line.
- Profile: one title, a caption row for where data lives, then cards (account, names, first home) and rows with chevrons; Remove ads is a row, not the only filled button.

## Pushed screen (settings, a sub-page)
- 54 · nav row 62 to 106 with the back caret · hairline at 113 · screenTitle at 126 · helper · content 24 under · footer button if the screen saves anything (pinned, never floating mid-page).
- Region and units: a preview card showing a price, an area, a distance and a time in the chosen units; "Scores don't change. Only how things show."; a row for the country; disclosure rows whose values are chips.
- Remove ads: the title, a helper, "Ads are not personalised" caption, benefits as rows, one pinned button that carries the store price (never a typed number).
- Sign-in: "Not now" at the top left, a card saying where data lives until sign-in, provider buttons 16 apart, an "or" rule, the email field with its caption straight above its own button.

## Sheet
- Scrim, the sheet from its content height up: grabber, title row with a close button, helper, content, footer pinned inside. 20 pt inset. Full width.
- Status: rows 56 with the status chip; opens high enough to show every status.
- Fix a fact: the field with a "You typed" badge under it; no doubled label.
- Keep with a note / Reject: a 96 to 120 note area, a privacy caption, 44 pt reason chips 8 apart, the button pinned; no Cancel (the close button is the cancel).
- Which house? / Add a priority: a title, one helper, rows (compact house rows, or 56 rows with a provenance badge at the right).
- Why 80?: the score card (ring, "66% known" under it, "52.8 ÷ 66 = 80%" and "52.8 of 66 known points" beside it), the Known gloss, "How the points add up · Tap a step", steps as rows with a full-width bar and the tapped step's explanation inside its row, a Total row, Got it.
- Before you start (permissions): three rows (camera, microphone, location) with one line each and one button; the phone asks for each on first use.

## Empty state
- The screen's own title and helper, then one message (two lines at most) and one button that is the way on. The helper is the empty one ("Your houses show here once you add one."), not the populated one. Never 600 pt of cream under a segmented control.
- Empty Home: the header, "Add your first house" as a section header with its helper as caption, the four one-tap ways-in cards, a rule, then "Your priorities are ready" as a row.

## Detail page (the House page)
- 240 hero placeholder with floating Back and Share · street as screenTitle at 20 under the hero · suburb line · price with the status chip 12 under · the score card 20 under · the Known gloss as a caption 8 under · "No viewing planned · Add a time" as a row · "What we know" as a disclosure row · "Check at the viewing (1)" as a section header with its caption · "Start viewing" pinned.
- The score inside the first 500 pt. The score said once: in the ring. "2 of 3 priorities known. 1 to check at the viewing." beside it.
- Scrolled: one header style with a rule; notes and viewings as rows; the agent questions as numbered rows with "Add a question" as a row (its form folded); Comments with "No comments yet." and a composer.

## Forms
- Labels 8 above fields; helpers 6 under; sections 24 apart; chips for a few choices in a 2 by 2 grid (44 tall) when they would wrap; sliders on the content edge; the optional part behind a row; the button pinned and disabled until the one required thing is set.
- When the keyboard is up, the footer button sits on the keyboard and the fields scroll above it.

## Field screens (Inspection Mode)
- A close button at the left and a brightness toggle at the right instead of the nav caret; the title, the helper, the section progress (three 4 pt bars), a short address line, a hairline.
- Rating as five pips; the count slot always present ("No red flags yet"); ticked rows show their follow-up (a photo prompt, the answer field) under them; the footer holds Prev, the one filled Next/Finish, and Note and Photo as 44 pt outlined squares.
- Camera off is a state of its own: icon, "Camera off", one line, "Open phone settings", "Add a note instead"; the controls that need the camera hide.

## Compare
- Pick: title, one caption, rows with ticks, the count on the pinned button; pricing rules are not explained here.
- Table: the winner card on street names with its one reason and a full-width "Go for this one"; a Table/Cards control; a compact column header (56 thumb, street, "score · label", known) so two priority rows show above the fold; the Known gloss once.
- Report: a "Blur faces and papers" switch row above the preview card; the note above the button.

## Dark twins
Every screen has one. Draw it with the dark tokens only; check the hero buttons, chips, badges and map tiles (tiles stay light); the raised + and the filled button use primaryFill, text links use primary.
